## 1. What Spybee sells today: products, platform modules, customers, funding, team, geography

### Takeaway
Spybee is a small (~18–19 people) Bogotá-founded ConTech startup (founded ~2018) selling a cloud construction-control platform built around visual reality capture (360° cameras, drone photogrammetry/orthophotos, timelapse cameras) plus incidents/tasks/RFIs, schedule/progress control, BIM/ACC integration and, since v3.0 (launched Dec 1, 2025), AI agents and WhatsApp-driven communication. It operates mainly in Colombia (>200 projects), with projects in Chile and pilots in Mexico since late 2025; funding is very small (pre-seed, Leonard/VINCI and Buildtech Ventures).

## 2. Spybee's LiDAR / point cloud / Scan-to-BIM practice: equipment, software, deliverables, turnaround

### Takeaway
Spybee publicly advertises terrestrial LiDAR scanning and dense classified point clouds only within its topography/survey service line (with DJI RTK drones and total stations), delivering DWG/RVT/PDF/SHP files and "topography modeled in Revit" for BIM integration. No public evidence was found of a building-level Scan-to-BIM service (architectural/structural/MEP as-built Revit models), of scanner brands used, or of turnaround times — this must be treated as not publicly available.

### Cited Findings
- Field capture on the topography site: "Drones DJI con cámaras RTK calibradas" and "Escaneo LiDAR terrestre para alta densidad"; for large or hard-to-access areas they complement with drone aerophotogrammetry and terrestrial LiDAR — [Spybee Topografía](https://topografia.spybee.com.co/)
- Heritage work: "Captura no invasiva con LiDAR terrestre" — [Spybee Topografía](https://topografia.spybee.com.co/)
- Data products: georeferenced orthomosaics, digital terrain/surface models (MDT/MDS), "Nube de puntos densa y clasificada" (dense, classified point cloud) — [Spybee Topografía](https://topografia.spybee.com.co/)
- Deliverables: certified planimetry and altimetry DWG, reports and certifications; files in .dwg, .rvt, .pdf, .shp — [Spybee Topografía](https://topografia.spybee.com.co/)
- "Como valor agregado, modelamos la topografía en Revit para integración con proyectos BIM" (topography modeled in Revit as added value) — [Spybee Topografía](https://topografia.spybee.com.co/)
- Claims alignment with Colombian authorities/reviewers (IGAC, DIGT, acueducto/water utility, curadurías urbanas) and that deliverables pass review without observations (marketing claim) — [Spybee Topografía](https://topografia.spybee.com.co/)
- Platform lets users measure distances, areas and volumes on BIM models and point clouds — [Spybee home (EN)](https://home.spybee.com.co/en/)
- Real-time Autodesk Construction Cloud integration claimed by co-founders — [Julian Rico LinkedIn](https://co.linkedin.com/in/julian-rico-spybee/en)
- 360 capture hardware: Spybee supplies a 360 camera for >1-year contracts (HoneyBuild 360, ~2023) — [Spybee LinkedIn](https://co.linkedin.com/company/spybee/). No brand (Insta360, Ricoh Theta, Matterport, etc.) appeared in any Spybee result.
- Searches restricted to Spybee domains for "nube de puntos / escaneo láser / BIM as-built / Insta360 / Ricoh / Matterport" returned no blog article on laser-scan-to-BIM or as-built building modeling — [Spybee blog search results incl. Drones vs. Cámaras 360](https://www.spybee.com.co/blog/drones-vs-camaras-360)

### Inferences
- Spybee's current "scan-to-BIM" is most likely a manual, service-based pipeline: terrestrial LiDAR + drone capture → registration/processing in vendor software → manual drafting in AutoCAD/Civil 3D and manual Revit modeling (at least of topography/toposurfaces). Revit (.rvt) is confirmed as a deliverable; the processing software (e.g., Cyclone, Trimble RealWorks, Pix4D, DJI Terra, Agisoft, ReCap) is not disclosed.
- Because Spybee already has (a) LiDAR capture capability, (b) a cloud viewer that handles point clouds and BIM models, and (c) an ACC/Autodesk link, an automated Scan-to-BIM product would logically extend from topographic surfaces to building elements (walls, slabs, columns, openings, MEP) and push results to ACC/Revit.
- The "pass curaduría review" positioning suggests deliverables must comply with local cadastral/urban-licensing conventions (DWG plans, certified surveys), so automated outputs will likely still need DWG plan sheets in addition to RVT/IFC.

### Gaps
- Scanner make/model (Leica, FARO, Trimble, NavVis, GeoSLAM, etc.), processing software, accuracy specs (mm), point density, LOD offered, typical turnaround and pricing: not publicly available in any result found.
- Whether Spybee has delivered building as-built Revit models (architecture/structure/MEP) for any client: no public evidence found.
- Whether the platform's point-cloud viewer supports E57/LAS/RCP upload or only Spybee-generated photogrammetric clouds: unknown.

## 3. Colombia's national BIM strategy: mandates, timelines, and as-built/existing-asset requirements

### Takeaway
Colombia's Estrategia Nacional BIM 2020–2026 (led from the Presidency/DNP) phased BIM into public projects (10–25% → 35–50% in 2023 → 60–75% in 2024 → 85–100% in 2025), culminating in a 2026 national-level mandate for national-order and state-co-financed public construction projects; the transport sector has required BIM since 2024. National BIM use guides (Camacol; UPIT) explicitly include "existing conditions survey" and "as-built model / asset management" uses, but no source found makes scan-based as-built models of existing buildings mandatory.

### Cited Findings
- Official BIM portal of the Presidency describes the phased requirement: BIM on 10–25% of public construction projects in early years, 35–50% in 2023, 60–75% in 2024, 85–100% in 2025, and 2026 as the national BIM mandate — [bim.presidencia.gov.co](https://bim.presidencia.gov.co/)
- 2020–2021 was a preparatory phase in which entities had to design an organizational transformation plan; a Medellín press release says the 10–25% requirement started January 1, 2022 (sources disagree on whether the first phase started 2020 or 2022) — [ACIS: ABC de la metodología BIM](https://acis.org.co/portal/content/abc-de-la-metodolog%C3%ADa-bim-en-colombia); [ACIS: BIM se exigirá](https://acis.org.co/portal/content/desde-este-a%C3%B1o-la-metodolog%C3%ADa-bim-se-exigir%C3%A1-en-los-proyectos-de-construcci%C3%B3n-p%C3%BAblicos-del)
- 2026 scope: ACIEM Valle reads it as covering all public construction projects of national order or co-financed by the national government — [ACIEM Valle](https://www.aciemvalle.org/aciem-valle-lidera-la-transformacion-digital-del-gremio-de-la-ingenieria-ante-la-obligatoriedad-del-uso-de-bim-en-proyectos-publicos/); Universidad Católica frames it more broadly as 100% of public construction and infrastructure projects from 2026 — [Universidad Católica de Colombia](https://www.ucatolica.edu.co/portal/que-pasara-con-la-obra-publica-en-colombia-en-2026-con-la-obligatoriedad-bim/) (broader reading likely overstated)
- Transport sector has had a BIM requirement in force since 2024; national/co-financed projects mandate in 2026 — [Antorr Academia](https://antorr.co/academia/estrategia-nacional-bim-colombia.html)
- Strategy goal: at least 10% cost-efficiency savings on public investment projects by 2026 — [Da Vinci Ingeniería](https://www.davinci.com.co/innovacion/estrategia-bim-en-colombia/)
- Strategy document indexed internationally as "Estrategia Nacional BIM 2020-2026" — [GlobalBIM](https://globalbim.org/info-collection/national-bim-strategy-2020-2026/)
- Camacol's "Guía de Usos BIM" (v3) includes "levantamiento de condiciones existentes" (existing-conditions survey) and "gestión de activos" (asset management) uses, the latter integrating the BIM model with CMMS/BMS/space-management systems, owned by the asset/facility manager — [Camacol Guía de Usos BIM V3 (PDF)](https://camacol.co/sites/default/files/descargables/Usos%20BIM%20V3.pdf)
- UPIT (Unidad de Planeación de Infraestructura de Transporte) BIM uses guide (Dec 2023) defines an "As-Built model" use to monitor the state of the facility and its assets — [UPIT Guía de Usos BIM (PDF)](https://upit.gov.co/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/201223-Guia-de-Usos-BIM.pdf)
- COBie cited as the usual exchange format for asset management in BIM — [Espacio BIM: As Built](https://www.espaciobim.com/as-built)
- Laser scanning and as-built modeling are promoted as the way to digitize existing buildings into a BIM 7D (facility management) strategy — [The Factory School](https://thefactoryschool.com/blog/bim-7d-facility-management/)
- Trade press: Estrategia Nacional BIM set progressive targets and contemplates BIM mandatory nationally in 2026 — [En Obra](https://www.en-obra.com/es/noticias/metodologia-bim-lo-que-viene-para-el-sector-constructor-en-colombia)

### Inferences
- The 2026 mandate mainly drives BIM for new design/construction of public works; demand for Scan-to-BIM comes indirectly via (a) renovation/rehabilitation/expansion of existing public buildings and infrastructure (where existing-conditions models are a listed BIM use), (b) as-built handover for asset management, and (c) contractors needing to verify built work against design models.
- Since the strategy's horizon ends in 2026, a follow-on policy (post-2026 roadmap) may be under discussion; none was found.
- Public-sector clients will likely require IFC (openBIM) alongside RVT, given government interoperability emphasis — not directly verified.

### Gaps
- Primary DNP/Presidency strategy document and any decree/resolution text were not opened; the precise legal instrument for the 2026 obligation is unverified.
- No BIM Forum Colombia document was found in results.
- No source found requiring point-cloud-based as-built models (or specifying LOD/accuracy for existing-conditions models) in Colombian public contracts.
- Whether a post-2026 national BIM strategy exists as of late 2026: not found.

## 4. Competitors and providers: Scan-to-BIM / LiDAR services and reality-capture platforms in Colombia and LatAm, with prices/turnaround

### Takeaway
The Colombian Scan-to-BIM market is served by small surveying/BIM consultancies (e.g., SIGTOP with Trimble X7, Kinedria in Bogotá, Arplan, BIM BCD, Da Vinci Ingeniería, Productividad360) and by hardware distributors (Leica via Grupo Acre, Latam GeoSystems, Casa del Topógrafo). Spybee's direct platform competitors in LatAm are mostly timelapse/visual-monitoring firms (Lisual, OBRALiVE, Fotomanager, FlyVideo) rather than OpenSpace/Buildots, whose LatAm presence was not confirmed. No Colombian per-m² Scan-to-BIM prices or turnaround times were found; international 2026 benchmarks run roughly US$0.10–0.80/ft² for typical scan-to-BIM.

## 5. Regional constraints: equipment import costs, BIM modeler availability and cost, outsourcing, deliverable formats, language

### Takeaway
Imported scanners face Colombian import VAT of 19% on CIF + duty, with duties 0–35% depending on HS subheading (often reduced via FTAs with the US/EU). Revit/BIM modelers in Bogotá earn roughly COP 3.5–4.5M/month for mid-level roles (floor ~COP 2–2.5M on per-project contracts), which is cheap by US standards but comparable to or above Indian outsourcing rates (US$10–18/h). Expected deliverables are RVT, IFC, DWG (plus E57/RCP point clouds), with Spanish-language workflows.

### Cited Findings
**Import costs**
- Importing to Colombia involves customs duty (0%–35%), VAT 19% on CIF + duty, and antidumping where applicable; FTAs (US, EU, others) can reduce duty with a valid certificate of origin; an illustrative chapter-84 industrial equipment example carries 5% duty (not confirmed for scanners) — [Alegra 2026 import tax guide](https://blog.alegra.com/colombia/tributacion-de-importaciones-en-colombia/)
- Import VAT base = customs value (incl. freight and insurance) + duty (Art. 459 E.T.), at the general 19% rate — [Gerencie](https://www.gerencie.com/cuando-se-causa-el-iva-en-las-importaciones.html)
- Courier de minimis (≤US$200 from US) does not apply to survey-grade scanners; above it, 10% duty + 19% VAT for courier — [Alegra](https://blog.alegra.com/colombia/tributacion-de-importaciones-en-colombia/)

### Inferences
- At an assumed ~COP 4,000–4,200 per USD (exchange rate not sourced here), COP 3.5–4.5M/month ≈ US$850–1,100/month base salary, i.e., roughly US$5–7/hour before employer social charges (prestaciones/seguridad social, typically a significant uplift; not verified here) — competitive with or below Indian outsourcing rates (US$10–18/h vendor billing rates), so local human-in-the-loop QA of automated models is economically viable in Colombia; the case for automation is more about speed, scale and consistency than pure labor arbitrage.
- Survey-grade terrestrial scanners (Leica RTC360/BLK, Trimble X7, FARO Focus) are bought through local distributors with 19% VAT and possible duty on top of USD prices, raising capex; this favors (a) cheaper capture (iPhone/iPad LiDAR, 360 photogrammetry, SLAM handhelds) for progress-type use and (b) device-agnostic ingestion (E57/LAS) so clients or partner surveyors can supply scans.
- An automated service for Colombia should output RVT (Autodesk-dominant market, ACC integration) plus IFC (public-sector openBIM), and DWG plan sheets for curaduría/permit workflows, with Spanish-language UI/reports.
